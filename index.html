01 — The Studio

A studio devoted to the quiet art of living well.

LUMORA STUDIO designs interiors and architecture that feel deeply personal — places where proportion, light and material come together to reflect the people who inhabit them.

We work with homeowners, property developers and businesses from the first sketch to the final styled detail. Every commission begins with listening: how you move through a day, what you keep close, and what you hope a space will make possible. From there, we shape environments that are restrained in form and rich in feeling.

04 — Our Design Philosophy

We design rooms that feel inevitable — as though they could never have been any other way.

Good design rarely announces itself. It is felt in the way morning light crosses a floor, in the weight of a door handle, in the ease of moving from one room to the next. Our work is an exercise in editing: removing what is unnecessary until only what matters remains.

  1. i.

    Light before objects

    We begin with daylight and shadow. Furniture, colour and finish follow the way a space is lit across a day.

  2. ii.

    Honest materials

    Stone, timber, linen and metal are chosen for how they age. We favour surfaces that grow more beautiful with use.

  3. iii.

    Quiet proportion

    Scale and rhythm create calm long before decoration does. We refine the bones of a room until it feels effortless.

05 — Design Process

From first conversation to final reveal.

A clear, unhurried path in four stages — so you always know where your project stands and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We listen first. Together we explore how you live or work, walk the site, and define the ambitions, budget and timeline for the project.

    You receiveProject brief & site study

  2. 02

    Concept Development

    Ideas take shape through sketches, material palettes and spatial studies, presented as a clear narrative for your space.

    You receiveConcept presentation & moodboards

  3. 03

    Design & Planning

    The concept is resolved in detail: layouts, joinery, lighting and specifications, coordinated with consultants and makers.

    You receiveDrawings, schedules & specifications

  4. 04

    Final Transformation

    We oversee the build and installation, then style every room — so the finished space is ready to be lived in from day one.
